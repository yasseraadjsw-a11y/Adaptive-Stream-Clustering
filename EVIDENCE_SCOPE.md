# Experimental evidence scope

All reported values are results of the actual study executions. Directory and file names changed during manuscript revision; these organizational labels do not define different experimental provenance.

- Proposed, TWStream, and FRA-ART were evaluated directly on CoverType, Electricity, TweetEval, and Synthetic GMM under the same study environment and protocol.
- Proposed, Fixed Rank, TWStream, and FRA-ART were additionally evaluated on the same controlled representation-drift stream.
- Controlled and ablation folders retain seed-level records; the multi-dataset folders retain the corresponding executed study result tables.
- The rank folder records the dataset-specific adaptive-rank analysis requested during review.
- Verification utilities check the integrity and cross-document consistency of the executed study record.
